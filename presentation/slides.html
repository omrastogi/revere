REVERE

Boston PD runs roughly 1,200 public cameras that record everything and understand nothing. Revere sits on top of the cameras the city already has — and turns them into real-time safety intelligence.

Camera count: Boston Globe (2024) ~1,300 · ACLU/City report 1,153 (Jul 2024)

2
The idea

One AI detector. Two audiences.

  • ▸Residents  see a live crime map of Boston neighborhoods — Roxbury, Dorchester, Mattapan — no login.
  • ▸Officials  review every AI detection with its clip and verify or dismiss it.
  • ▸Privacy by design  — only human-verified incidents ever reach the public.
